01 / About

A short note on me

I'm a Software Development Engineer at AWS Agentic AI, based in Seattle. I'm a founding member of Quick Research — an autonomous deep-research service — where I designed the RAG architecture that grounds the agent in a customer's own organizational data, and built the secure tool-use integrations that let it query third-party financial providers like FactSet and S&P.

Before AWS, I spent four and a half years at Amazon Customer Service, working across the chat platform that handles 100% of global customer service traffic — chat initiation APIs, the migration from a rules engine to a GenAI-powered chatbot, and an LLM-based silent CSAT scorer. Before that: Citrix R&D in Bangalore for nearly four years, building product analytics and translation tooling.

I hold three U.S. patents in secure file sharing and offline content streaming, and I finished my MS in Computer Science at USC Viterbi in 2020.

Outside work, I take photographs, play a few musical instruments, and spend most weekends swimming or on a basketball court. I served as Membership Head at the IEEE Student Branch, Manipal — leading a team of twelve to record-breaking membership numbers.
